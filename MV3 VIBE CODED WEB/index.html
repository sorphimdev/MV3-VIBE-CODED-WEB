01 01Identity

Max Emilian Verstappen

Born September 30, 1997 in Hasselt, Limburg, Belgium, and raised just up the road in Maaseik, close to the Dutch border. He holds both Belgian and Dutch nationality — but spent more time with his father on the racing side of the family growing up, and chose to compete under the Dutch flag.
